# Algae Farm

A Portuguese browser game for a Biochemical Engineering class about microalgae, biodiesel, and whole-biomass or starch-based bioplastics.

[Play Algae Farm](https://candidomolino.github.io/algae-farm/)

Version 0.5.1 opens in untimed free play. Introduce the game, then let students explore, discover hidden details, retry contracts and compare routes at their own pace. The countdown is hidden by default. Teacher controls retain optional timed rounds through explicit minutos=6 or minutos=10 links. Existing saved timed rounds retain their settings; new games default to untimed play.

Three weather profiles and three operating conditions are revealed before the affected choice. Shared scenario codes repeat conditions across pairs. Reports explain the effects and comparisons identify matching conditions. Three optional discoveries fill a field notebook, including a hidden EEL/USP emblem.

[Fixed reference scenario](https://candidomolino.github.io/algae-farm/?cenario=fixo) preserves the original teaching quantities. The 14 process turns and resource limits remain part of the simulation. Model 0.2.0 safely restores earlier saves.

[Download editable Piskel and Tiled art sources](https://candidomolino.github.io/algae-farm/Algae_Farm_Art_Source.zip). Terrain and small props: Kenney [Tiny Town](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town) and [Tiny Farm](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm), CC0. The separate EEL/USP institutional logo comes from the [official library visual identity page](https://www.bibliotecas.eel.usp.br/servicos/identidade_visual). Original project artwork supplies the researcher, aerators, algae equipment and interface.

The self-contained HTML needs no account or external runtime service. All quantities and event chances are teaching conventions, not measured industrial performance.
